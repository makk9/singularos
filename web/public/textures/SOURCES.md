# Earth texture sources

These 2048 × 1024 maps are historical satellite composites used for an
illustrative scene. They do not represent current weather or city lighting.
The files are bundled locally; the demo does not fetch imagery from NASA at runtime.

## Surface

- File: `earth-day.png`
- Source: [Blue Marble — A Seamless Image Mosaic of the Earth](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/)
- Download: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png
- Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA's Earth Observatory.
- This composite includes surface, ocean, ice, and cloud imagery. A separate
  cloud shell adds illustrative motion and shading; it is not a weather model.

## Clouds
